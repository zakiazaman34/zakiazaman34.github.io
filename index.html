I am a researcher working on privacy-preserving machine learning for IoT data. I completed my PhD at UNSW Sydney in December 2025, supervised by Prof. Wen Hu, Prof. Sanjay Jha, and Dr. Praveen Gauravaram, focused on privacy-preserving machine learning for IoT data, through probabilistic data encoding, differential privacy, and LLM-based forecasting for smart-meter data. I bring over five years of university teaching experience alongside my research, and I welcome opportunities to collaborate.

About

My PhD research designed a privacy-preserving probabilistic data encoding technique for IoT datasets, supporting both classification and forecasting while balancing data utility against privacy inside deep learning pipelines. I also proposed Bloom-LLM, a framework that uses large language models for privacy-preserving load forecasting on smart-meter data, and developed a differentially private synthetic data generation method to support safe downstream analysis of the same datasets — implemented and evaluated in TensorFlow and PyTorch, with UMAP, Matplotlib, and Seaborn used throughout for visual analysis of privacy–utility trade-offs.

Since completing my PhD, I've worked as a Postdoctoral Writing Fellow at UNSW Sydney, preparing journal manuscripts and conference papers and extending my thesis work into new research directions. Before my PhD, I spent over five years as a Lecturer (Senior Scale) at Daffodil International University, Dhaka, teaching Data Structures, Algorithms, Object-Oriented Programming, and Digital Electronics, and advising more than 300 students.

I am an Australian citizen based in Sydney, and I'm currently seeking academic and research positions in privacy-preserving machine learning.
