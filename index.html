I am a researcher working on privacy-preserving machine learning for IoT data, through probabilistic data encoding, differential privacy, and LLM-based forecasting for smart-meter data. I completed my PhD at UNSW Sydney in December 2025, supervised by Prof. Wen Hu, Prof. Sanjay Jha, and Dr. Praveen Gauravaram. I bring over five years of university teaching experience alongside my research, and I welcome opportunities to collaborate.

01 · Overview

About

I'm a Sydney-based researcher building privacy-preserving machine learning systems for IoT data — completing my PhD at UNSW in December 2025, and now working as a Postdoctoral Writing Fellow. Before that, I spent five-plus years lecturing in Computer Science at Daffodil International University, Dhaka.

01

Encoding

A probabilistic data encoding technique for IoT datasets, balancing utility and privacy across classification and forecasting tasks.

02

Forecasting

Bloom-LLM — a large-language-model framework for privacy-preserving smart-meter load forecasting.

03

Synthesis

Differentially private synthetic data generation, preserving statistical properties for safe downstream analysis.
